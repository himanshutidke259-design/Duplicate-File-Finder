Responsible Use

Use AI as a Tool for Thinking, Learning, and Creating.

ChatGPT can be useful, but it is not infallible. Its responses may contain errors or outdated information. Verify important claims, protect personal and confidential information, respect intellectual property, and follow relevant academic and professional rules.

Use AI thoughtfully and apply your own judgment.

Yes. The guide is written in beginner-friendly English and introduces practical ChatGPT applications without requiring a technical background.

No programming background is required to begin reading the guide. Coding is one of the topics covered, but the book also explores writing, learning, productivity, content creation, and business applications.
